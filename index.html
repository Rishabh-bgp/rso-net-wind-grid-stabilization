道 How it works

Dilated causal convolutions (a TCN) and self-attention produce P10/P50/P90 quantile forecasts; the band can drive an advisory, human-approved reserve suggestion. The model predicts the 10-minute change in wind speed and adds it to the current reading.

Weights were trained in PyTorch, exported to JSON and re-implemented in plain JavaScript. A test checks that the JS and Python predictions match within 1e-4.
